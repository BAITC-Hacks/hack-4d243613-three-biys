# Consent to the Collection and Processing of Personal Data

2. **Purposes.** Publishing tasks, maintaining the catalog, sending and reviewing proposals, communication between businesses and student teams, recording milestones and points, operation of the AI assistant, security of the platform.

3. **Actions with the data.** Collection, recording, systematization, storage, modification, use, dissemination within the platform, depersonalization, blocking and destruction.

7. **Minors.** If I am under 18, the consent is given on my behalf by my legal representative.

The consent is given electronically by ticking a box on the platform. The Operator keeps a record of the date and version of this consent.
