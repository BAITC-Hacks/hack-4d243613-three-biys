## 2. Definitions

2.1. **Platform** means the Көпір web service at {{siteUrl}}.

2.2. **Business representative** means a User who publishes a task on behalf of an organization or an individual entrepreneur.

2.3. **Student team** means a group of Users who browse tasks and send proposals together. A team acts through its team profile.

2.4. **Task** means a description of a business need, problem or assignment, set out as a card.

2.5. **Task card** means the structured description of a task: title, context, need, users, data, constraints, expected result, success criteria, contact and format of interaction.

2.6. **Readiness rating** means a score from 0 to 100 that shows how ready a task is for work with students. It is calculated under the Catalog and Rating Rules.

2.7. **Catalog** means the shared list of published tasks available to all student teams.

2.8. **Proposal** means a student team's offer on a task: solution idea, plan, deadline and a link to a prototype.

2.9. **Milestone** means a part of the work on a task whose completion is confirmed by the Business representative.

2.10. **Team points** means the platform's internal points a team earns for milestones confirmed by the business.

2.11. **AI assistant** means the platform features based on artificial intelligence systems.

## 3. Users and authority

3.1. The Business representative confirms that they are authorized to act on behalf of the organization whose task they publish.

3.2. Members of a student team confirm that they have the necessary legal capacity. A member under 18 uses the platform with the consent of their legal representative.

3.3. The User provides accurate information and is responsible for keeping it up to date.

3.4. A team profile does not contain gender, age, ethnicity, religion, health or other sensitive information. It contains the team's name, description, interests, skills and technologies.

## 4. Publishing tasks

4.1. The Business representative describes the task in their own words. The AI assistant asks clarifying questions and proposes a draft card.

4.2. The Business representative reviews every field of the card, edits it if needed and confirms it personally. A card is published only after manual confirmation.

4.3. The Business representative is responsible for the content of the card. It is prohibited to publish:
- personal data of third parties without a legal basis;
- information that is a trade secret or another secret protected by law, without the decision of its holder;
- false information and advertising unrelated to the task;
- materials that violate the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan or the rights of third parties.

4.4. Students receive synthetic or depersonalized data for their work. Real data is shared only under a separate agreement of the parties and where a legal basis exists.

## 5. Moderation

5.1. The Operator reviews published materials automatically and manually. The automatic review looks for personal data and signs of violations of these Terms.

5.2. If material violates these Terms or the law, the Operator may hide it, return it for revision or delete it. The User receives a message with the reason.

5.3. The User may request a review of a moderation decision by writing to {{operatorEmail}}. The repeated decision is made by an employee of the Operator.

## 7. Rating and catalog

7.1. The readiness rating is calculated by software under an open formula. Points are awarded only for card fields that are filled in and confirmed.

7.2. The rating reflects the readiness of a specific task. The fame, size or status of a company does not affect it.

7.3. The rating determines the task's position in the catalog. A task with a low rating stays in the catalog with a "needs clarification" mark and can still receive proposals.

7.4. The rating is informational. The Operator does not guarantee that a task will receive proposals.

## 8. Proposals and choosing a team

8.1. Any student team may send a proposal on any published task. The number of proposals is not limited.

8.2. The Business representative compares the proposals and decides to choose one team, several teams or none. Neither the platform nor the AI assistant chooses or assigns teams.

8.3. The Business representative states the period within which they review proposals. The platform shows this period to student teams and marks tasks where it has been missed.

8.4. Choosing a team does not oblige the parties to enter into a transaction. The parties agree on the terms of joint work in a collaboration agreement. The platform provides a template of the agreement; the Operator is not a party to it.

## 9. Milestones and team points

9.1. Points are awarded to a team only after the Business representative has confirmed that a milestone is completed.

9.2. Team points reflect confirmed progress and are used within the platform. Points are not money, have no monetary value and cannot be transferred to third parties.
